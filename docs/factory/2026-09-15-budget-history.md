# Audit budget history

Daniel asked Ada to consider frequent substantial budget edits and generate a new audit for comparison. The live account's 90-day record ending 2026-09-14 contains 254 readable budget edits across 49 objects, with 86 in the last 30 days. Seventeen objects meet the recurrence screening rule, ten within the recent month. A general status log had crowded these receipts out, and the Tinkers parser did not recognize composite payment envelopes.

`budget-history.ts` groups deduplicated dated before/after budgets by object ID, measures moves, seven-day recurrence and reversals within 48 hours, and carries explicit coverage. Three edits including two moves of 20% or more within seven days is an analytical rule only. It does not count or confirm learning resets. From-zero moves have no percentage. Missing data does not establish inactivity. The direct non-Tinkers source does not yet carry readable budget amounts and explicitly reports this analysis unavailable.

The Tinkers bridge requests the budget category separately, one provider page per call, at most 32 pages per 30-day window. Three windows run concurrently. Safe cursors only, no continuation URLs; repeat, missing, unsafe and failed continuations retain earlier data as partial. Unreadable budgets remain partial even if later pages finish. The memoized result feeds learning and account activity, and dated receipts also enter root-cause analysis. Weekly event volume no longer proves learning status or forces consolidation.
